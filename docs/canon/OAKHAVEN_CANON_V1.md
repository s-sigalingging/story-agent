# OAKHAVEN CANON v1.0

**Canon Version:** 1.0  
**Universe:** OAKHAVEN  
**Present Day:** 2026  
**Status:** FROZEN
**Authority:** Primary creative specification for the Oakhaven universe.

---

# 1. CANON PURPOSE

OAKHAVEN CANON v1.0 defines the authoritative creative boundaries of the Oakhaven universe.

It exists to ensure that stories, characters, locations, clues, assets, and generated production material remain consistent across episodes.

This document defines creative truth.

Machine-readable representations derived from this document must preserve its meaning and may not silently introduce new canon.

---

# 2. CANON AUTHORITY

The authority hierarchy is:

OAKHAVEN CANON
→ approved character/world data
→ approved story and episode state
→ production planning
→ production assets
→ prompts
→ generated outputs

When legacy material conflicts with Canon v1.0, Canon v1.0 wins.

Valid information statuses are:

- HARD_CANON
- SOFT_CANON
- OPEN
- LEGACY
- DEPRECATED
- NON_CANON

OPEN means that the answer has intentionally not yet been established.

OPEN information must not be converted into fact by a Story Author, validator, generator, or other pipeline component without explicit canon approval.

---

# 3. CORE STATEMENT

Oakhaven is a coastal city built on inherited lies, where every generation mistakes a fragment of history for the whole truth—and every attempt to bury the past creates the next crime.

---

# 4. CORE NARRATIVE PRINCIPLE

The city survives not because everyone knows the truth and hides it, but because every generation inherits only fragments of the lie.

INHERITED LIE does not imply MASTER CONSPIRACY.

Some people intentionally lie.

Some misunderstand evidence.

Some protect institutions.

Some follow orders.

Some destroy evidence without understanding its significance.

No modern character or faction automatically possesses complete historical knowledge.

---

# 5. WORLD IDENTITY

Oakhaven is a fictional coastal industrial city.

Primary genre identity:

- Historical mystery
- Crime mystery
- Noir
- Investigative drama

The world uses grounded historical realism.

Oakhaven is not a supernatural universe.

Apparently supernatural events must remain explainable through human activity, environmental conditions, misinformation, psychology, historical misunderstanding, or other material causes.

---

# 6. HISTORICAL LAYER I — 1856

## THE ILLEGAL FOUNDATION

Status: HARD_CANON

The foundation of modern Oakhaven involved the unlawful seizure of land belonging to an existing fishing community.

Members of the early Pike family participated in manipulating ownership records and establishing apparently legitimate claims over disputed land.

This event forms the deepest currently established historical truth behind Oakhaven's central mystery.

Modern characters do not automatically know this truth.

---

# 7. HISTORICAL LAYER II — 1930

## THE INDUSTRIAL COVER-UP

Status: HARD_CANON

During 1930, workers encountered evidence connected to older land ownership and the foundation of Oakhaven.

They did not possess enough context to understand the complete historical truth.

Partial evidence was interpreted through contemporary industrial conflict, corruption, labor disputes, and local power structures.

Violence and suppression followed.

Records were fragmented, hidden, transferred, destroyed, or removed from their original context.

1930 is not the origin of Oakhaven's central lie.

1930 is an echo of 1856.

---

# 8. HISTORICAL LAYER III — 1970s

## THE BUREAUCRATIC ERASURE

Status: HARD_CANON

During Oakhaven's modernization in the 1970s, historical records were reorganized, reclassified, separated, suppressed, or manipulated.

Not every participant understood the historical significance of the material they handled.

This process created records that may be:

- miscatalogued,
- incomplete,
- missing pages,
- duplicated under different references,
- restricted,
- separated from contextual records,
- individually authentic while collectively misleading.

The previously referenced "1970 massacre" is DEPRECATED.

Canon v1.0 establishes bureaucratic suppression and civic abuses during the modernization period, but does not establish a separate major 1970 massacre.

---

# 9. HISTORICAL LAYER IV — 2026

## THE CURRENT TRAGEDIES

Status: HARD_CANON

Present-day crimes emerge when fragments of buried history are rediscovered.

The recurring mechanism is:

OLD LIE
→ FRAGMENT DISCOVERED
→ MODERN FEAR
→ SUPPRESSION
→ NEW CRIME
→ NEW EVIDENCE
→ OLDER HISTORICAL LAYER EXPOSED

Modern crimes therefore create new evidence while attempting to destroy old evidence.

---

# 10. KNOWLEDGE MODEL

Every significant fact may exist independently across four knowledge dimensions:

1. WORLD_TRUTH
2. CHARACTER_KNOWLEDGE
3. CHARACTER_BELIEF
4. AUDIENCE_KNOWLEDGE

WORLD_TRUTH describes what is actually true.

CHARACTER_KNOWLEDGE describes information a character has legitimately acquired.

CHARACTER_BELIEF describes what a character thinks is true, whether correct or incorrect.

AUDIENCE_KNOWLEDGE describes what the story has actually made available to viewers.

A character may not knowingly act upon information that they have not canonically learned.

Pipeline components must not transfer WORLD_TRUTH automatically into CHARACTER_KNOWLEDGE.

---

# 11. NO OMNISCIENT VILLAIN RULE

Status: HARD_CANON

No character or faction currently possesses complete knowledge of Oakhaven's historical truth.

This applies to:

- Pike Elite
- Oakhaven Police Department
- Custodians
- Waterfront Collective
- Shadow Operatives

Characters may possess dangerous fragments of truth.

They may incorrectly believe those fragments constitute the entire truth.

Oakhaven's central mystery must not collapse into a single all-knowing mastermind unless a future canon revision explicitly changes this rule.

# 13. TRAVEL RULES

Status: HARD_CANON

Pike Hill to Old Docks requires approximately 30–40 minutes by normal road travel.

Blackwood's Tideland Path is accessible approximately between 01:00 and 04:00 during low tide.

An old coal railway provides an illegal transportation connection between Central Depot and the Old Docks.

Characters must not teleport between locations.

Story timing must respect meaningful travel constraints.

# 17. WEATHER RULES

Permanent gloom and overcast maritime atmosphere are recurring world characteristics.

Fog, drizzle, and wind are conditional environmental states.

OAKHAVEN IS NOT ALWAYS RAINING.

Rain or drizzle must not be added automatically to every scene.

Weather continuity must be maintained within connected scenes.

# 18. THE 04:00 FOG

Sea fog around the waterfront is a recurring environmental phenomenon around the early-morning period, particularly near 04:00.

Visibility can become extremely limited.

This creates opportunities for hidden movement, evidence disposal, crime, and misidentification.

The fog is not supernatural.

It functions as:

- environmental constraint,
- criminal opportunity,
- recurring visual motif.

# 19. FACTIONS

Five major faction groups form the current social structure.

## FACTION_PIKE_ELITE

Old-money power network centered around Pike Hill and associated institutions.

Members protect status, legitimacy, wealth, or inherited authority.

They do not automatically know the truth about 1856.

## FACTION_OPD

Oakhaven Police Department.

Internally divided between institutional preservation, corruption, obedience, and genuine investigation.

## FACTION_CUSTODIANS

People connected to archives, records, memory, information, and preservation.

Their access to information does not guarantee correct interpretation.

## FACTION_WATERFRONT

Working-class waterfront network.

Possesses oral history, community memory, rumor, and local knowledge that official records may omit.

Oral history is valuable but not automatically accurate.

## FACTION_SHADOW_OPERATIVES

People involved in illicit transportation, cleanup, intimidation, destruction, or concealment.

Operational involvement does not imply knowledge of the historical conspiracy.

# 23. PL-1930

Canonical Identifier: PL-1930  
Status: HARD_CANON

Deprecated Identifier:

A-1930

A-1930 may exist only as a legacy alias.

New canonical stories must use PL-1930.

PL-1930 is an archival/catalog identifier associated with surviving material and evidence connected to the 1930 historical layer.

Its apparent meaning changes as additional context is discovered.

Expected knowledge progression may include:

strange identifier
→ archival reference
→ 1930 evidence cluster
→ evidence pointing toward older land history
→ connection toward 1856.

PL-1930 is not a magical single document that explains the entire mystery.

It may refer to or connect multiple surviving records, physical materials, transfers, catalog entries, or related evidence.

# 24. DOCUMENT RULE

Documents in Oakhaven may be:

- authentic,
- forged,
- incomplete,
- miscatalogued,
- deliberately separated,
- incorrectly interpreted,
- copied inaccurately,
- physically damaged,
- partially destroyed,
- missing context.

An authentic document does not guarantee an accurate interpretation.

This rule directly challenges Julian Vance's initial worldview.

# 25. CLUE SYSTEM

Oakhaven uses five clue levels.

LEVEL 1 — VISUAL

Physical or visible evidence.

It should create a question rather than immediately explain the mystery.

LEVEL 2 — BEHAVIORAL

Actions, reactions, habits, avoidance, timing, or inconsistencies in behavior.

LEVEL 3 — DIALOGUE / TESTIMONY

Statements, rumors, testimony, contradiction, omission, or verbal information.

LEVEL 4 — LOCATION / ENVIRONMENT

Geography, tide, fog, access routes, environmental traces, architecture, or spatial evidence.

LEVEL 5 — SYSTEMIC / HISTORICAL

Institutional patterns, historical structures, archival systems, inherited power, and multi-generational evidence.

# 26. FAIR-PLAY RULES

## RULE OF VISIBILITY

Evidence required for a resolution must have been made available to the audience before that resolution.

## RULE OF MISDIRECTION

The audience may be encouraged to interpret evidence incorrectly.

The story must not manufacture a twist merely by hiding evidence already known to the investigator.

## PAYOFF PRINCIPLE

Resolving one mystery should expose another unresolved layer.

A payoff should deepen understanding while creating a meaningful next question.

---

# 27. EVIDENCE-BASED DEDUCTION

Important deductions require observable evidence.

Example:

CLAIM:
Three pages are missing.

VALID EVIDENCE:
Page numbering jumps from 142 to 146.

Without supporting evidence, a precise deduction must not be treated as established fact.

Story validators should reject unsupported detective knowledge.

---

# 28. MYSTERY PATTERN

Default Oakhaven mystery progression:

OBSERVATION
→ QUESTION
→ INITIAL THEORY
→ CONTRADICTION
→ NEW CONTEXT
→ REINTERPRETATION
→ PARTIAL ANSWER
→ DEEPER QUESTION

Avoid mystery construction based primarily on:

→ unsupported twist.

Every episode must meaningfully alter character or audience knowledge state.

- slow push,

---

# 42. IDENTITY VISIBILITY RULE

A character whose face is too small or ambiguous in a wide keyframe creates identity risk during image-to-video camera movement.

Production planning may therefore split a visual progression into multiple shots or keyframes.

Example:

WIDE STATIC SHOT
→ CUT
→ MEDIUM CHARACTER SHOT

is preferred over forcing an I2V model to invent facial detail while moving from an indistinct distant face into a close view.

Wide shots remain valid and important for geography, atmosphere, pacing, and visual storytelling.
